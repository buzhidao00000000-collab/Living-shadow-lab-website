About

Living Shadow Lab connects ideas, funding, production, and shadow puppet artists.

We turn heritage innovation into something users can join, support, learn, and co-create.

Who we are

A co-creation platform for young audiences, creators, shadow puppet artists, and offline cultural spaces.

The problem

Shadow puppetry needs fresher stories, younger participation, steadier artist income, and clearer learning paths.

How it works

The script pool collects ideas, users vote, artists estimate costs, and the platform launches crowdfunding.

User actions

Users can submit ideas, vote, back shows, watch premieres, join workshops, and grow into co-creators.

Artist value

Artists gain new scripts, production funds, premiere traffic, teaching revenue, sales channels, and young collaborators.

Platform value

The platform turns one-time viewing into ongoing participation, real purchases, learning paths, and artist support.
